## Facts measured this morning that the code now depends on

- **`/clear` writes the session transcript immediately**, holding only
  `custom-title`, `agent-name`, `mode` and `bridge-session` records. So the
  file's absence is not a test for a fresh session and its presence is not
  a test for a used one. The first version of RIG-41 shipped on the absence
  test and two live builders disproved it an hour later.
- **Which of the rig record and the session registry is stale was measured
  both ways on consecutive days.** The registry was fresher on the 12th
  (RIG-33: a reset re-read it before the runtime had rotated the id); the
  rig record was fresher at 11:42 AM on the 13th, written the moment the
  reset typed `/clear`. The gauge now prefers neither and takes whichever
  names the newer transcript.
- The session registry's `tmux` field is `"<session>:@<window>.%<pane>"`.
  The pane id is a stricter key than the declared name: it cannot collide
  across rigs and it is right before a rename lands. Two records can claim
  one pane after a relaunch; the newer `startedAt` wins.
- **Asking for a checkpoint costs about 35K.** Builder 1, 140K ceiling:
  asked at 100,503 it peaked at 143,970 before its reset; asked at 114,055
  it peaked at 146,631. The pane finishes its turn, writes up to 12,000
  characters, files them and prints its marker, and each is a full-context
  request. This is the arithmetic behind "100K is too small": at 100K a
  flat 80 percent threshold lands the reset at about 115K.
- **A pane can cross the whole threshold between two passes.** Builder 1
  read 43 percent at 11:50:47, 72 at 11:51:47 and 102 at 11:54:18. Its
  fastest stretch was 18 percent to 67 percent in 75 seconds. A shorter
  interval cannot fix that; projecting the rate can.
- `kern.memorystatus_vm_pressure_level` sat at `warn` for ten unbroken
  minutes on a Mac with 3 GB free and a load of 3.5 on ten cores. `warn`
  is a working machine. Only `critical` raises the alarm on its own.
- One `launchd_sim` process is one booted simulator, for free.
  `xcrun simctl list` costs about a second and must not be in a watch pass.
- An operator client attached at 83 columns shrinks a 240-wide rig's
  builders to 39 and 43. The narrow pane is the symptom; the client is the
  cause, and both are now printed together.
